# Guilded Studios

**Bringing communities together.**

Guilded Studios is building a community communication platform focused on making it easier for people to connect, collaborate, and spend time together.

Our goal is to create a welcoming home for communities of every size, with the communication tools people need in one place.

## What we're building

The platform is being developed around the needs of online communities, including:

- **Text chat** — conversations organized around topics and channels.
- **Voice and video** — ways for communities to talk and hang out.
- **Communities** — spaces for groups, teams, friends, and shared interests.
- **Direct messages** — simple conversations between people.
- **Community tools** — features that help people organize and manage their spaces.

*These are project goals; features and availability may change as development progresses.*

## Development

Guilded is currently a work in progress. We are building the foundation, exploring features, and shaping the experience before a public release.
